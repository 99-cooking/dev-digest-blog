# SEA Fintech Weekly — Feb 28 – Mar 6, 2026

## 🎯 TL;DR

The big story this week: **Money20/20's Future of Fintech in APAC report** dropped ahead of Bangkok's April conference, and the headline is unmistakable — SEA is the #1 expansion target for APAC fintech (22.9% of executives), AI adoption has crossed the tipping point (61.2% already deployed), and fraud prevention has overtaken growth as the top operational priority (63.5%). Meanwhile, APAC fintech funding hit a **decade-low** at $9.3B in 2025 according to KPMG, but the region is stabilizing rather than falling — setting up a leaner, more disciplined 2026 cycle.

## 🏦 NEOBANK & DIGITAL BANKING

**1. APAC Neobanking to Grow at 30.46% CAGR Through 2031**
Mordor Intelligence projects neobanking as APAC's fastest-growing fintech segment, expanding from the current base to a $348.1B total fintech market by 2031. Digital payments still dominate at 64.93% market share, but neobanks are where the growth momentum lives. This reflects regulatory tailwinds in SG, HK, AU, and PH where digital bank licenses have opened the floodgates. ([Asian Banking & Finance](https://asianbankingandfinance.net/financial-technology/in-focus/neobanking-grow-3046-cagr-in-apac))

**2. MariBank Philippines Profitable for Two Consecutive Years — Assets Hit $1.16B**
Sea Group's MariBank Philippines (technically a rural bank with digital-centric model, NOT a licensed digital bank under BSP's framework) reported breakeven for both FY2024 and FY2025. Total assets grew from ~$636M in Q1 to $1.16B by end of 2025. President Siew Ghee Kung Lim called it proof that "the digital banking model can be sustainable in the Philippines." This is significant — most SEA digital banks are still hemorrhaging cash. ([Fintech News PH](https://fintechnews.ph/70334/banking/maribank-philippines-profitability-asset-growth-2025/))

**3. GoTyme Pulls Kiosks from Pick n Pay (South Africa), Shifts to Channel Agnosticism**
GoTyme Bank is withdrawing kiosks from Pick n Pay stores in South Africa by March 31, pivoting to a multi-channel distribution strategy. After rapid growth in both the Philippines and South Africa, the bank is reducing dependency on single retail partners and expanding into independent merchant locations. Smart move — low-cost acquisition via shared physical infra. ([Frontier Fintech](https://frontierfintech.substack.com/p/frontier-fintech-gps-62-march-4th))

**4. BSP Tightening Standards for Digital Banks — "Fintech Storm Brewing"**
ABS-CBN reports the BSP is raising the bar for digital banking licenses as rural banks increasingly rebrand as digital banks. Expect stricter capital requirements and compliance standards. The six licensed PH digital banks (GoTyme, UnionDigital, Overseas Filipino Bank, Tonik, UNObank, Maya Bank) remain the only officially recognized players. ([ABS-CBN](https://www.abs-cbn.com/news/business/2026/2/26/a-fintech-storm-is-brewing-2005))

**5. Philippines Digital Bank Interest Rate Competition Heats Up**
March 2026 data shows PH digital banks still competing aggressively on savings rates: Tonik hit 2M customers within three years offering 6% annual savings rates. Google Pay/Wallet launched in PH (Nov 2025) with partners including GCash, Maya, GoTyme, UnionBank, RCBC, Wise, and Zed Financial — expanding tokenized in-device payments. ([BitPinas](https://bitpinas.com/fintech/digital-banks-high-interest-rates-philippines/), [GlobeNewsWire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/02/3247603/28124/en/Philippines-Prepaid-Card-and-Digital-Wallet-Market-Intelligence-Report-2026.html))

**6. Malaysia Digital Bank Executive Exodus Continues**
Multiple C-level departures at Malaysian digital banks in recent months, driven by relentless profitability pressure. The contrast with MariBank PH's two-year breakeven streak is stark — suggests Malaysia's digital banking experiment is entering a reckoning phase. ([Fintech News MY](https://fintechnews.my/56073/digital-banking-news-malaysia/))

**7. Revolut Expanding Aggressively — India Waitlist Opens, $75B Valuation**
Revolut ($75B valuation after $2B raise in Nov 2025) opened India waitlists and is hiring senior regulatory/compliance leaders across 20+ countries simultaneously. While the immediate focus is India, this systematic multi-market entry approach signals SEA expansion is on the roadmap. CB Insights gave Revolut a perfect hiring momentum score of 100. ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/fintech-predictions-2026/))

## 💸 PAYMENTS & REMITTANCE

**1. ShopeePay Launches Cross-Border QR Payments via Alipay+ — 150M+ Merchants**
Sea Group's ShopeePay enabled overseas QR payments for Singapore travelers through Alipay+ integration, covering 150M+ merchants across 100+ markets. This is ShopeePay's first cross-border payments offering and positions it as a serious travel wallet competitor in SG. ([Fintech News SG](https://fintechnews.sg/127241/payments/shopeepay-overseas-qr-payments/))

**2. QRIS Expanding to South Korea — Launch Target April 2026**
Bank Indonesia and Bank of Korea agreed to implement QRIS cross-border payments launching April 2026. QRIS is already live with Thailand, Malaysia, and Singapore. BI Deputy Governor confirmed India and Saudi Arabia are next on the 2026 expansion roadmap. Foreign tourists (especially from MY, SG, TH) are increasingly using QRIS at Indonesian SMEs. ([Kompas](https://money.kompas.com/read/2026/02/27/085000926/qris-tembus-korea-selatan-babak-baru-ekonomi-digital-indonesia), [Sijori Today](https://sijoritoday.com/2026/03/06/qris-cross-border-mudahkan-wisman-beli-produk-umkm-tempatan/))

**3. TrueMoney Thailand: Cross-Border Spending in China Up 82%**
TrueMoney reported Thai users' cross-border spending grew 45% overall, with China specifically surging 82%. The platform launched a "Travel" service category and deepened its Alipay partnership for cross-border payments, including credit-first-pay-later options for travel. ([Brand Buffet](https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2026/02/truemoney-insights-thais-spending-in-china-82-percent-growth-partnering-alipay-for-cross-border-payments/))

**4. Project Nexus: Philippines "Ahead" of Peers — Mid-2027 Go-Live**
BSP Deputy Governor Tangonan confirmed the Philippines is leading Project Nexus implementation: key executives hired, Singapore entity established, technology operator selected, platform being built. Go-live target: mid-2027. Initial participants: PH (InstaPay), MY (DuitNow), TH (PromptPay), SG (PayNow), ID (BI-FAST), India (UPI). ([BitPinas](https://bitpinas.com/fintech/bsp-project-nexus-instapay))

**5. Malaysia Logged 11.8M Cross-Border QR Transactions (H1 2025)**
Worth RM967 million — concrete proof that ASEAN QR interoperability is moving real money, not just pilot volumes. ([Kapronasia](https://kapronasia.com/insight/blogs/payments-research/how-cross-border-qr-payments-are-reshaping-asias-digital-economy))

## 📋 REGULATORY & POLICY

**1. BSP Mandates Server-Side Biometrics, Phases Out SMS OTPs**
The BSP is directing financial institutions to replace SMS/email OTPs with server-side biometric authentication for high-risk transactions. Institutions failing to maintain adequate security will be liable for customer losses from scams. Those compliant will be shielded from certain cybercrime liability. This is aggressive and forward-looking — most countries still rely on OTP. ([Fintech News PH](https://fintechnews.ph/70395/security/bsp-mandates-server-side-biometrics-authentication-philippines/))

**2. OJK Sanctions 22 Fintech Lenders + 17 Multifinance Companies in February**
Indonesia's OJK handed out administrative sanctions (written warnings) to 22 P2P lending operators, 17 multifinance companies, and 2 venture capital firms during February 2026. Also evaluating license applications from 2 crypto exchanges, 2 clearing institutions, 2 custodians, and 4 digital asset trading firms. The licensed fintech lending count remains at 95 operators. ([Kontan](https://keuangan.kontan.co.id/news/ojk-beri-sanksi-kepada-17-multifinance-dan-22-fintech-lending-pada-februari-2026))

**3. Hong Kong to Issue First Stablecoin Issuer Licenses in March**
HKMA will grant the first fiat-backed stablecoin issuer licenses this month under a strict regime. While not SEA, this sets a regulatory precedent that Singapore and Thailand will likely reference. SFC and FSTB are planning 2026 legislation for virtual asset dealers and custodians. ([Crypto News](https://crypto.news/hong-kong-to-license-fiat-stablecoins-tighten-va-dealers-and-custody/))

**4. Philippines E-Payments Hit ₱24.74 Trillion in 2025 (+42% YoY)**
Combined e-payment value now represents ~90% of Philippine GDP. This isn't adoption — it's domination. The growth rate justifies BSP's aggressive digitization push and the tightening of security standards.

## 🌏 COUNTRY SPOTLIGHT

### Philippines — The SEA Fintech Overachiever

The Philippines had the most action this week, and it's not close:

- **BSP mandating biometrics over OTPs** — the most aggressive auth security move in SEA
- **Project Nexus leadership** — Philippines is ahead of peers in connecting InstaPay globally
- **E-payments at ₱24.74T (+42%)** — representing 90% of GDP
- **MariBank profitable for 2 years** — first sustainable digital bank proof point in SEA
- **6 licensed digital banks** competing on rates (Tonik at 6% savings)
- **Google Pay launched** with major wallet/bank partners
- **BSP tightening digital bank standards** — weeding out rural banks trying to rebrand

Why it matters: The Philippines is building the most complete digital banking ecosystem in SEA — regulated, competitive, increasingly profitable, and now with cross-border infrastructure via Project Nexus. For a market with 70M+ unbanked adults, this is transformational.

### Indonesia — The QR Payments Juggernaut

- **QRIS cross-border expanding to Korea** (April 2026), then India and Saudi Arabia
- **OJK sanctioning** 22 fintech lenders — regulatory discipline increasing
- **OJK evaluating crypto licenses** — exchanges, custodians, trading platforms
- **Honest raised $140M** — credit card issuer proving Indonesia fintech can still raise big
- **Cross-border QRIS usage surging** among MY/SG/TH tourists at Indonesian SMEs

Indonesia's play is infrastructure-first: make QRIS the QR standard for all of ASEAN (and beyond), then layer financial services on top.

## 🔮 TRENDS & PREDICTIONS

**1. Stablecoins Are Coming to SEA Payments — Fast**
Pine Labs' stablecoin prepaid card (SEA launch by April), Visa x Bridge expanding to 100+ countries, HK licensing stablecoin issuers — the infrastructure for stablecoin-based payments in SEA is being built right now. Within 12 months, expect at least one major SEA e-wallet to integrate stablecoin settlement for cross-border transactions.

**2. The Great Digital Bank Reckoning of 2026**
Malaysia's C-level exodus, BSP tightening PH standards, KPMG's decade-low funding numbers — the era of "raise money, burn cash, acquire users" is over. 2026 is the year digital banks must prove unit economics or die. MariBank PH's profitability proof point will separate believers from skeptics.

**3. QR Interoperability Becomes ASEAN's Killer Feature**
QRIS expanding to Korea/India/Saudi, ShopeePay x Alipay+ covering 150M merchants, Project Nexus targeting mid-2027 go-live — ASEAN is building the world's most integrated real-time cross-border payment network. By 2027, a single QR scan will move money between 6+ countries. This is the infrastructure moat that makes SEA fintech unique globally.

**4. Security Becomes the Differentiator**
BSP's biometrics mandate, 63.5% of leaders citing fraud as #1 priority, OJK sanctions wave — regulators are shifting from "promote growth" to "protect consumers." Fintechs that invest in security and compliance now will have a competitive advantage when the next wave of regulation hits.

## 💡 OPPORTUNITY RADAR

**1. Stablecoin-Native Remittance for SEA Migrant Workers**
With Pine Labs and Visa building stablecoin card infrastructure and remittance costs at 90% lower via stablecoins vs. Western Union, there's a massive gap for a SEA-focused stablecoin remittance app targeting the 10M+ Filipino OFWs and millions of Indonesian, Vietnamese, and Myanmar migrant workers. The BSP is already welcoming of InstaPay cross-border connectivity — a stablecoin on-ramp that connects to InstaPay could be the winning formula.

**2. Fraud-Prevention-as-a-Service for SEA Digital Banks**
63.5% of fintech leaders say fraud is their #1 concern. BSP is making institutions liable for customer losses. Yet most SEA neobanks are building security in-house with limited AI capabilities. A B2B fraud intelligence platform purpose-built for SEA (understanding local scam patterns, Bahasa/Tagalog/Thai language processing, regional phone number patterns) would find immediate demand.

**3. SME Cross-Border Payment Infrastructure**
Export-oriented manufacturers in Vietnam, Thailand, and Malaysia are adopting alternative payment solutions to escape 3-5 day correspondent banking settlement. QRIS cross-border is consumer-focused. There's no dominant B2B cross-border payment platform purpose-built for SEA SMEs. Airwallex plays here but from an enterprise angle — the mid-market SME space is underserved.
