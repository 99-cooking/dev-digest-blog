# SEA Fintech Weekly — May 23–29, 2026

## 🎯 TL;DR

Grab's consolidation of Superbank marks a watershed for embedded banking in SEA — the superapp now controls a profitable, 6M-user digital bank in Indonesia with $1.4B in assets. Meanwhile, Tonik became the first standalone digital bank in the Philippines to hit profitability, and stablecoins are crossing from crypto curiosity into real payment infrastructure as Circle partners with Singapore-based Nium for USDC settlement across 190 countries. The anti-fraud front is intensifying region-wide, with both Indonesia's OJK and Vietnam's SBV launching coordinated scam-fighting platforms.

---

## 🏦 NEOBANK & DIGITAL BANKING

**Grab Takes Majority Control of Superbank (Indonesia)**
Grab completed the transfer of Singtel Alpha's stake in Superbank to GXS Bank, pushing its combined ownership past 50%. Starting May 2026, Superbank's financials will be consolidated into Grab's Financial Services segment. The bank — which listed on IDX in December 2025 under ticker SUPA — now serves 6M+ customers with 1M+ daily transactions. Assets grew 72% YoY to IDR 24 trillion ($1.4B), net interest income up 84%, and it posted its first full-year profit in FY2025. About 60% of Superbank customers also use Grab/OVO. This is the clearest example yet of the super-app-to-bank pipeline actually working.
→ [Marketing Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/grab-to-consolidate-superbank-following-stake-transfer-to-gxs-bank) · [DailySocial](https://dailysocial.id/p/superbank-joins-grabs-books-vinfast)

**Tonik Hits Profitability — First Standalone Digital Bank in PH**
Tonik, the Philippines' first standalone digital bank (License #001), reported consolidated positive cash net income in Q1 2026 after all costs including cost of risk. This makes it the first standalone digital bank in the country to reach sustained profitability. A meaningful milestone given the skepticism around digital bank unit economics in emerging markets.
→ [Hubbis](https://www.hubbis.com/news/tonik-becomes-the-first-standalone-digital-bank-in-the-philippines-to-achieve-profitability)

**CLICX Enters Thailand's Virtual Banking Arena**
CLICX has officially entered Thailand's virtual banking market, targeting underbanked consumers — freelancers, gig workers, and small vendors — with alternative credit scoring based on utility payments, mobile wallet habits, and transaction data rather than traditional credit bureau scores. Thailand's BOT has been laying the groundwork with 80% adult financial account ownership and PromptPay's 67M+ users.
→ [Chiang Rai Times](https://www.chiangraitimes.com/finance/clicx-shakes-up-thailands-virtual-banking/)

**Sea Limited Q1 2026: Revenue Up 46.6%, SeaMoney Driving Growth**
Sea Ltd reported Q1 2026 revenue of $7.1B (+46.6% YoY), gross profit of $3.1B, and net income of $438M. SeaMoney (fintech arm including ShopeePay and Monee) continues expanding across SEA and into Brazil. Shopee hit record orders. Barclays maintained an Overweight rating. EPS missed analyst estimates, suggesting competitive pressure on margins, but the top-line trajectory is powerful.
→ [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/sea-limited-se-outlook-strengthens-114948426.html)

**Bank Rakyat Launches Fintech Community Hub (Malaysia)**
Bank Rakyat partnered with FAOM, PayNet, and Plug and Play to create a Fintech Community Hub — standardising how Islamic cooperatives and fintechs collaborate. This is notable because it targets the cooperative banking segment, which serves many underbanked Malaysians.
→ [Fintech News MY](https://fintechnews.my/58749/various/bank-rakyat-fintech-community-hub/)

**Revolut Crosses 70M Global Customers**
Revolut now has 70M+ customers globally (as of March 2026). While its current focus is European expansion (Hungary IBANs, Portugal hub, 5M Italian customers) and an aggressive Australian push, the global neobank remains one to watch as a potential SEA entrant. Its scale and $100B+ valuation make it a credible threat to any regional player.

## 💸 PAYMENTS & REMITTANCE

**Circle × Nium: USDC Settlement Goes Global**
Singapore-based cross-border payments company Nium partnered with Circle to enable USDC stablecoin settlement across 190+ countries via Coinbase APIs. This is arguably the most significant stablecoin-meets-traditional-payments infrastructure deal this year. For SEA, where Nium is deeply embedded in the cross-border flow, this creates a viable stablecoin-to-fiat-to-payout pipeline.
→ [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/partnerships/2026/circle-and-nium-launch-stablecoin-settlement-partnership/) · [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/circle-nium-usdc-global-payouts/)

**RCBC × EzyRemit: Philippines-Australia Corridor**
RCBC partnered with Australia-based EzyRemit to expand digital remittance services between the Philippines and Australia. PH personal remittances grew 2.3% YoY to $3.2B in March 2026, but that growth rate is slowing. RCBC also won two Digital CX Awards for its Pulz platform, citing innovations in remittances and virtual USD accounts.
→ [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/business/banking/2026/05/29/2531407/rcbc-eyes-stronger-philippines-australia-payment-flows-through-ezyremit-deal)

**Cross-Border QR: Philippines Joins the Party**
Philippine e-wallet users can now scan Thailand's PromptPay and Malaysia's DuitNow QR codes directly. This bilateral QR interoperability is expanding across ASEAN, cutting card network fees and enabling competitive FX rates. Laos also completed PromptPay cross-border QR linkage.
→ [Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/05/24/business/sunday-business-it/the-power-of-the-collaborative-ecosystem-scaling-fintech-in-the-philippines/2350479)

**Wise Adapts to Thailand's PromptPay Integration**
Wise is rolling out PromptPay features for Thailand users, creating a smoother on-ramp for foreign fund recipients. Users who immediately transfer to Thai bank accounts experience zero disruption. This further cements PromptPay as the rails everyone must plug into.

**Maybank Completes Secure2u Migration (Malaysia)**
Maybank finished migrating all online banking services — including bill payments, DuitNow, FPX, and IBG — to its Secure2u authentication system. Security hardening of Malaysia's largest bank's digital channels.

## 📋 REGULATORY & POLICY

**Indonesia's OJK Launches National Fraud Portal (IASC)**
OJK is building a National Fraud Portal within the Indonesia Anti-Scam Center (IASC) to enable faster fraud case handling through integrated report collection, information exchange, and transaction tracking. Separately, Operation FRONTIER+ (March–May 2026) was a cross-border anti-scam operation. Total fraud losses flagged: Rp 13,229 trillion. Victim funds blocked: Rp 614.3 billion.
→ [Antara News](https://en.antaranews.com/amp/news/416851/indonesia-launches-new-anti-scam-platform-through-ojk)

**Vietnam's Anti-Fraud System Blocks $177M in Suspicious Transfers**
Vietnam's SBV revealed its SIMO system has flagged 1.3M+ suspicious transactions and prevented $177M in potentially fraudulent transfers. The system alerts users but doesn't block transactions — a soft-touch approach. However, participation among banks remains "strikingly limited." SBV announced Digital Finance Day 2026 to promote awareness.
→ [VietnamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/vietnam-anti-fraud-banking-system-blocks-177-million-in-suspicious-transfers-2519899.html)

**Indonesia Fintech Lending Hits Rp 101 Trillion — Youth Debt Alarm**
OJK data shows fintech lending outstanding reached Rp 101.03 trillion ($6.1B) as of March 2026. Non-performing loans (90+ days overdue) rose to 4.52%, up from 2.77% a year ago. Nearly 49% of overdue loans belong to borrowers aged 19–34. The term "pinjol" remains associated with predatory lending despite OJK's rebranding to "pindar."
→ [Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/opinion/2026/05/23/analysis-rp-101-trillion-fintech-loans-raise-youth-debt-concerns.html)

**MAS Stablecoin Framework: Circle Gets MPI License**
Circle holds a Major Payment Institution (MPI) license from MAS for Digital Payment Token services, making it the first global stablecoin issuer compliant with Singapore's Payment Services Act. MAS continues to position Singapore as the institutional hub for regulated digital assets in Asia.

## 🌏 COUNTRY SPOTLIGHT

### Indonesia: The Grab-Superbank Moment

Indonesia is this week's clear protagonist. Three stories converge:

1. **Grab's Superbank consolidation** validates the embedded banking thesis. A super-app with ride-hailing, delivery, and payments data can underwrite credit better than traditional banks. 60% customer overlap with Grab/OVO gives Superbank a near-zero customer acquisition cost for its most valuable users.

2. **Fintech lending's Rp 101T outstanding** with rising NPLs among youth (4.52%) signals the market is maturing fast — perhaps too fast. OJK faces a tension between promoting financial inclusion and containing predatory lending risk.

3. **The IASC National Fraud Portal** launch shows OJK taking anti-scam seriously at an infrastructure level, not just enforcement. Operation FRONTIER+ blocked Rp 614B in victim funds.

**The big picture:** Indonesia has 280M people, ~50% unbanked, and a $1.1T GDP growing at 5%+. Every major SEA fintech player — Grab, GoTo, Sea, Tonik — is fighting for this market. The winners will be those who can lend profitably to the informal economy without creating a youth debt crisis.

### Vietnam: Quiet Progress

Vietnam flew under the radar but delivered real substance: SBV's SIMO anti-fraud system has blocked $177M, the banking sector held a major digital transformation competition (won by Vietcombank), and the country is connecting fintech with green finance initiatives. Vietnam seeks UK expertise for green finance and international capital mobilisation. With 100M people and rising smartphone penetration, Vietnam remains the SEA market most likely to surprise in 2027–2028.

## 🔮 TRENDS & PREDICTIONS

**1. Embedded Banking Is Winning Over Standalone**
Grab's Superbank success (profitable, fast-growing, 60% customer overlap) vs. the struggles of standalone digital banks to acquire users cheaply. The super-app-to-bank pipeline is the dominant strategy in SEA. Watch for GoTo to accelerate its banking play in response.

**2. Anti-Fraud Infrastructure Becoming a Competitive Moat**
Both Indonesia (IASC/National Fraud Portal) and Vietnam (SIMO) are building centralized anti-fraud platforms. As scam losses escalate globally, countries with better fraud infrastructure will attract more fintech investment and consumer trust. Singapore's MyInfo API is the gold standard.

**3. Stablecoins Are Entering SEA Payments For Real**
Circle's MAS license + Nium partnership creates the first credible stablecoin-to-fiat settlement pipeline at scale. DealStreetAsia calls SEA "increasingly a proving ground for stablecoin-based payments." This is no longer a crypto story — it's a payments infrastructure story.

**4. Youth Debt Crisis Could Trigger Regulatory Crackdown**
Indonesia's fintech lending NPLs among 19–34 year-olds are rising fast. If this continues, expect OJK to impose tighter lending caps, interest rate ceilings, or mandatory financial literacy requirements. BNPL providers across SEA should be watching closely.

## 💡 OPPORTUNITY RADAR

**1. Anti-Fraud-as-a-Service for SEA Banks**
Both Indonesia and Vietnam are building national fraud portals, but bank participation remains limited (Vietnam's SBV explicitly noted this). A B2B SaaS product that makes it trivially easy for banks and fintechs to integrate with these national fraud databases — with real-time alerts, case management, and reporting — would find immediate demand across the region.

**2. Cross-Border QR Payment Aggregator**
ASEAN QR interoperability is expanding bilaterally (PH↔TH, PH↔MY, TH↔LA), but there's no single aggregation layer for merchants who want to accept all ASEAN QR schemes. A middleware play that normalizes DuitNow, PromptPay, QRIS, and PayNow for cross-border merchants could capture significant transaction volume as intra-ASEAN travel rebounds.

**3. Alternative Credit Scoring for Indonesia's Informal Economy**
With 49% of fintech NPLs coming from young borrowers and OJK's reputational headache around "pinjol," there's a gap for a credit intelligence provider that can score Indonesia's informal workers using utility data, e-wallet transaction history, and telecom data — exactly what CLICX is attempting in Thailand. The lender who cracks this earns the right to serve Indonesia's 140M unbanked adults.
