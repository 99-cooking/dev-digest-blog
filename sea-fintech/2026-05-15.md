# SEA Fintech Weekly — May 9–15, 2026

## 🎯 TL;DR

Tonik just became the first standalone digital bank in the Philippines to hit sustained profitability — proving the credit-led neobank model works in emerging SEA markets. Meanwhile, Indonesia and China officially linked their QR payment networks (QRIS ↔ Alipay+/UnionPay), the biggest cross-border payment corridor launch this year. Sea Limited crushed Q1 with $7.1B revenue (+47% YoY) and SeaMoney's loan book up 71%. And ASEAN nations are racing to finalize the Digital Economy Framework Agreement (DEFA) targeting a $2 trillion regional digital market.

## 🏦 NEOBANK & DIGITAL BANKING

**Tonik hits profitability — first standalone digital bank in the Philippines to do so** — On May 12, Tonik (BSP Digital Banking License No. 001) announced consolidated positive cash net income in Q1 2026. Unlike GCash-backed Maya or government-owned OFBank that hit profitability earlier, Tonik did it without a telco, payments, or retail ecosystem feeding it customers. CEO Greg Krasnov's thesis since 2020: emerging-market digital banking is a credit problem, not a user-acquisition problem. Globally, ~75% of neobanks remain unprofitable. This is a signal for every challenger bank in SEA. ([Insignia Review](https://review.insignia.vc/2026/05/13/tonik-profitability/) · [Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/05/12/tmt-newswire/pr-newswire/tonik-becomes-the-first-standalone-digital-bank-in-the-philippines-to-achieve-profitability/2341289))

**GCash now 30% of Globe's pre-tax earnings** — Globe's Q1 2026 disclosure shows GCash (via Mynt) contributed ₱1.9 billion in equity earnings, up 8% YoY and 120% QoQ. GCash now represents 30% of Globe's net income before tax, up from 22% a year ago. The IPO runway is getting shorter — this kind of earnings contribution demands standalone valuation. AI chatbots now handle 70%+ of GCash customer inquiries. ([Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/business/2026/05/15/2527958/gcash-doubles-globe-profit-share-p19-billion) · [Unbox](https://unbox.ph/news/gcash-globe-earnings-call/))

**GoTyme eyes third market, Forbes ranks it #2 in Philippines** — Tyme Group is scouting for its third digital bank launch after South Africa and the Philippines. ([Fintech News MY](https://fintechnews.my/58566/videos/where-will-gotyme-launch-their-3rd-digital-bank/) · [Bilyonaryo](https://bilyonaryo.com/2026/05/13/maribank-gotyme-lead-forbes-best-banks-list-in-philippines/money/))

## 💸 PAYMENTS & REMITTANCE

**Indonesia–China QR payment link goes live** — Bank Indonesia and the People's Bank of China officially connected QRIS with Alipay+ and UnionPay International on May 11. Chinese tourists can now pay at Indonesian merchants with their domestic wallets, and vice versa. This is the first non-ASEAN heavyweight node in the QRIS network (Japan was first in Aug 2025). The geopolitical subtext: China is building a regional QR network to transact in local currencies, reducing USD dependency one scan at a time. ([VisaHQ](https://www.visahq.com/news/2026-05-11/cn/indonesia-links-its-qris-payment-network-with-china-letting-tourists-pay-in-yuan-with-a-local-app/) · [CEOWORLD](https://ceoworld.biz/2026/05/10/inside-china-and-indonesias-new-cross-border-qr-network-and-who-gains-most/) · [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/indonesia-and-china-launch-cross-border-qr-payment-linkage-via-alipay-and-unionpay))

**TransFi launches BizPay — cross-border payments via WhatsApp & Telegram** — Dubai-based TransFi went live with BizPay across Philippines, Malaysia, Vietnam, and Indonesia, targeting 65M+ SMEs. Businesses can collect in USD/EUR/USDT/USDC and pay out locally in IDR/PHP/VND/MYR/THB. Pricing starts at 0.15% on stablecoin rails, 0.99% for fiat. No app download needed — it runs inside messaging apps. This is the embedded finance thesis meeting the stablecoin thesis. ([Fintech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/05/13/transfi-launches-bizpay-for-asean-cross-border-pay/) · [Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/payments/transfi-bizpay-bring-cross-border-payments-whatsapp-and-telegram-65m-smes))

**Vietnam joins ASEAN interconnected QR network** — Vietnam is now live in the six-country ASEAN QR payment system (alongside Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Philippines, Singapore). Vietnamese travelers can pay in dong across five countries. BizTech Forum 2026 in Ho Chi Minh City highlighted this as a "silent financial revolution." BSP has set a July 2026 target for ASEAN instant cross-border remittance. ([DoItQR](https://doitqr.com/en/biztech-2026-cross-border-qr-payment-vietnam))

**PayRex integrates Google Pay in the Philippines** — Philippine payment processor PayRex added Google Pay for faster digital checkout. Small move, big signal: the global wallets (Google, Apple) are quietly embedding into SEA's local rails. ([Fintech News PH](https://fintechnews.ph/71400/digital-payments/payrex-digital-wallet-google-pay-online-payments/))

## 📋 REGULATORY & POLICY

**ASEAN pushes to finalize DEFA — targeting $2T digital economy** — Under Philippines' 2026 ASEAN chairship, member states are moving to finalize the Digital Economy Framework Agreement. The goal: harmonize business rules, remove technical barriers, and transform the $300B regional digital market into a $2T economy by 2030 for 670M people. Announced at Manila Tech Summit. ([Fintech Alliance PH](https://fintechalliance.ph/asean-moves-to-build-world-first-digital-economy-framework-targets-2-t-market-under-ph-chairship/) · [PIA](https://pia.gov.ph/news/manila-tech-summit-2026-backs-digital-economy-seamless-asean-trade/))

**Philippines: Fintech industry backs shifting online lending oversight from SEC to BSP** — The Fintech Alliance Philippines is supporting the proposed transfer of online lending platform (OLP) regulation from the Securities and Exchange Commission to the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas. The SEC itself endorsed the move. Context: abusive collection practices, privacy violations, and predatory fees from unregulated lenders are a growing crisis. BSP has the mandate and resources to properly regulate. ([Inquirer](https://business.inquirer.net/590211/fintech-firms-back-shifting-online-lending-oversight-to-bsp) · [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/business/2026/05/14/2527733/fintech-group-backs-bsp-oversight-online-lenders))

**Bank Indonesia and BNM sign bilateral MoU** — The two central banks signed a new MoU on May 11 strengthening cooperation across monetary policy, payment systems, financial stability, and Islamic finance. This deepens the QRIS-DuitNow cross-border payment corridor already live between the two countries. ([FundsForNGOs](https://news.fundsforngos.org/2026/05/11/bnm-bank-indonesia-sign-mou-to-strengthen-financial-ties/) · [Bank Indonesia](https://www.bi.go.id/id/publikasi/ruang-media/news-release/Pages/sp_2810126.aspx))

**OJK pushes Indonesian fintech lenders toward productive financing** — Indonesia's financial regulator reports fintech lending to productive sectors hit Rp 34.66 trillion ($2.1B) in March 2026, up 23.4% YoY. Total P2P lending outstanding reached Rp 101 trillion ($6.2B), up 26.25% YoY. OJK's 2025-2026 roadmap targets 40-50% productive lending share. ([Kontan](https://keuangan.kontan.co.id/news/ojk-catat-pembiayaan-produktif-fintech-lending-tumbuh-2340-per-maret-2026))

## 🔮 TRENDS & PREDICTIONS

**1. Stablecoin rails are eating B2B payments.** TransFi's BizPay at 0.15% on stablecoin rails vs. 0.99% fiat is a 6.6x price difference. Fasset's $51M raise, the Money20/20 Asia stablecoin focus, MAS letting banks hold stablecoins with lower capital requirements — the infrastructure layer is being built now. Expect every cross-border payment startup in SEA to add stablecoin settlement within 12 months.

**2. The Philippines digital bank race enters the profitability phase.** Tonik proved it can be done standalone. Now the pressure mounts on UNObank, UnionDigital, and others. GCash's IPO timeline tightens as earnings contribution becomes too large to ignore. Watch for M&A among the weaker digital banks — six licenses for this market may be too many.

**3. QRIS is becoming Asia's de facto QR standard.** Indonesia's aggressive linking strategy (ASEAN, Japan, now China) positions QRIS as the hub of a pan-Asian payment network. The BIS Project Nexus phased rollout in 2026 will formalize this. China's Alipay+ integration is the strategic play — Beijing wants local-currency settlement across SEA.

**4. Embedded insurance is the next embedded finance frontier.** Money20/20 Asia's big theme: insurance moving from supplementary service to core digital product. In SEA where insurance penetration is <5% in most markets, the opportunity is enormous. Watch GCash, GrabPay, and ShopeePay add insurance products aggressively.

## 💡 OPPORTUNITY RADAR

**1. SME cross-border payments via messaging apps.** TransFi's BizPay just validated the model — 65M SMEs, most already on WhatsApp/Telegram, none with good cross-border payment tools. The pricing arbitrage between stablecoin and fiat rails creates a real wedge. A SEA-native competitor could undercut even TransFi by integrating directly with local wallets (GoPay, GCash, etc.) rather than just bank accounts.

**2. Credit scoring for the newly-banked.** Tonik's profitability proves credit-led digital banking works. But the underwriting models are proprietary. A standalone credit scoring/decisioning API — built on SEA-specific data (e-wallet transactions, QRIS history, telco data) — could serve all six Philippine digital banks plus Indonesia's 100+ fintech lenders. OJK's push for productive lending creates regulatory tailwind.

**3. Identity & fraud prevention infrastructure.** VIDA's FraudShield launch at Money20/20 Asia highlights a critical gap: as digital financial services scale, so does fraud. Deepfakes, injection attacks, and synthetic identities are outpacing traditional biometric checks across SEA. An identity verification layer purpose-built for SEA's regulatory patchwork (different KYC rules per country) could become essential infrastructure.
