## 💡 TECHNIQUES & IDEAS

**Fast-Slow Training (FST) — Stanford/Berkeley**
Inspired by Kahneman's "Thinking, Fast and Slow." Instead of forcing everything into model weights, FST separates: slow brain (parameters via RL for deep reasoning) and fast brain (dynamically optimized prompts for task-specific heuristics). Results: 3x more sample-efficient, 70% less catastrophic forgetting, infinite plasticity in continual learning.
🔗 X: https://x.com/HowToAI_/status/2056731528781254692

**Boris Cherny's CLAUDE.md Anatomy**
The real divide: most people talk TO AI, a small group builds infrastructure AROUND AI. Key principles: plan before execution, split tasks into focused agents, track failures like engineering systems, verify before shipping, eliminate root causes not symptoms.
🔗 X: https://x.com/NainsiDwiv50980/status/2056765341922119681

**Claude Code Resource Bible — 54 Tools**
