## 🔮 EMERGING TRENDS

**The "Skills Economy" is Real**
Claude Code skills are becoming the new npm packages. Garry Tan's GStack, op7418's collection, everything-claude-code — the ecosystem is exploding. People are building, sharing, and composing agent skills like never before.

**Local-First AI Going Mainstream**
Gemma 4 on phones, Ollama powering free Claude Code, GLM-5.1 on Venice — the push toward zero-cost, local AI is accelerating. The gap between cloud and edge is closing fast.

**Agentic Video Production**
OpenMontage signals a shift: AI isn't just generating clips, it's orchestrating entire production pipelines — research, scripting, asset generation, editing, rendering. Sub-$1 production costs.

**Rust Coreutils Replacement Struggling**
UUtils devs publicly admitting their Rust rewrites of GNU coreutils are "so subpar I don't even have words." GNU's decades of edge case handling proves hard to replicate. Ubuntu shipping it anyway.
🔗 X: https://x.com/LundukeJournal/status/2041539465592623268

**x402 Protocol Moving to Foundation**
Coinbase's x402 payment protocol repo moved to an independent x402 Foundation — signal that agent-to-agent payments are becoming a standard, not a company project.
🔗 X: https://x.com/CoinbaseDev/status/2041286835666923959
