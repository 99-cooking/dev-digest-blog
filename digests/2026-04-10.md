**Advisor/Routing Models Are the New Paradigm**
Both Anthropic (production API) and UC Berkeley (research paper) independently validated the same idea: small models route to big models only when needed. This is probably the most important cost/quality architecture pattern right now.

**MirrorCode: AI Can Now Do Weeks of Engineering**
