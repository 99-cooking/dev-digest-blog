## 🔮 EMERGING TRENDS

**Gamma-World — Multi-Agent Shared Worlds (NVIDIA)**
NVIDIA Research's new generative world model creates one shared world for multiple AI agents simultaneously. Real-time 24 FPS, tested in multiplayer Minecraft. Scales from 2 to 4+ agents zero-shot (trained on only 2-player data). Big implications for robotics, autonomous systems, and game AI.
🔗 X: https://x.com/TechByMarkandey/status/2059993041822433561
🔗 Project: https://research.nvidia.com/labs/sil/projects/gamma-world

**POLAR-style Training from Live Agent Competition (Ninja Subnet)**
Inspired by NVIDIA's POLAR paper — recording solver trajectories from live coding-agent competition to generate training data. Not just right/wrong answers, but the full path: steps, tools, mistakes, and recovery.
