**OpenAI vs Anthropic: Two Paths to Agent Harness**
Excellent analysis: OpenAI bets on full automation (Codex replacing devs), Anthropic bets on collaboration (Claude Code as co-pilot) then absorbing the harness layer into "Managed Agents." Both racing to become the Agent OS.
