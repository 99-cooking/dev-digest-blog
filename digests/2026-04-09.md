## 🔮 EMERGING TRENDS

**Frontier Models Going Enterprise-Only**
Both Anthropic (Mythos) and OpenAI (unnamed cyber model) are gating their most powerful models to vetted enterprises. The era of "release everything to everyone" is ending for the most capable systems. This is the new playbook.

**Agent Orchestration Going Open Source**
Cabinet, Multica, oh-my-codex, oh-my-claudecode — the community is rapidly building open-source alternatives to proprietary agent orchestration. If you can't use the managed service, clone the pattern.

**Hermes-Agent's Rise**
NousResearch's self-learning local agent quadrupled stars in 3 weeks. The appetite for agents that run on your own hardware and improve over time is massive.

**Meta's Comeback (Closed-Source Pivot)**
Meta abandoned the Llama open-source strategy for Muse Spark. Nine months of infrastructure rebuilding, 10x compute efficiency over Llama 4. The biggest distribution moat in AI (1B+ users) now has a competitive model behind it.

**Software Stocks Under Pressure**
Mythos + Claude Cowork announcements are crushing software stocks. The market is pricing in a world where AI agents do significant portions of software work.
