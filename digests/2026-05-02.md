## 🔮 EMERGING TRENDS

**Terminal-Native AI is the New IDE**
Between Warp open-sourcing, gemini-cli, and Claude Code's growing dominance over Cursor — the trend is clear: AI is moving INTO the terminal rather than wrapping around IDEs. Developers want AI where they already work, not in yet another Electron app.

**Agent Orchestration Going Hierarchical**
Projects like OpenGoat and Google's agents-cli signal a shift from single-agent coding to multi-agent hierarchies. We're moving from "AI pair programmer" to "AI engineering team."

**The "Portable Skills" Problem**
Multiple projects (ai-skills, agents-cli) are trying to solve the same thing: making AI agent capabilities transferable across different coding assistants. This is becoming the next interop battleground.
