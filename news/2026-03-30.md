## AI & AGENTS

**Anthropic "Mythos" Leaked — Step Change in Capabilities**
An accidental data leak revealed Anthropic is testing a model it internally calls a "step change" in performance. Anthropic confirmed the leak and said Mythos is in testing. Why it matters: if it lives up to the internal framing, this could reset the frontier. Anthropic is also in IPO talks for Q4 2026, potentially raising $60B+ — second-largest ever behind SpaceX. Watch: pricing, access tiers, and whether this accelerates or delays the IPO.

**Apple Opens Siri to All AIs in iOS 27**
Bloomberg reported iOS 27 will let Gemini, Claude, and others compete inside the iPhone via a new Extensions system. Apple is also building its own chatbot (codenamed "Campos") powered by Google's Gemini. Why it matters: Apple just turned the iPhone into an AI marketplace. Distribution is everything — this makes the assistant war a platform war. Watch: which models ship at launch and how Apple handles data routing.

**OpenAI Killed Sora, Acquired Astral, Teased "Spud"**
Sora shut down after just 6 months, taking a $1B Disney deal with it. Meanwhile OpenAI made its 6th acquisition of 2026 (Astral, open-source dev tools). Sam Altman relinquished safety oversight to focus on data centers and fundraising. Why it matters: OpenAI is pivoting hard from consumer products to infrastructure. Six acquisitions in 3 months signals a platform consolidation strategy. Watch: what "Spud" turns out to be.

**Google's Biggest AI Day — Gemini 3.1 Flash Live + Search Live**
Google launched Gemini 3.1 Flash Live, rolled out Search Live in 200+ countries, and added chat history import from rival AI apps. Why it matters: Google's bet is making Gemini so sticky you never leave. The chat history import feature is a shrewd lock-in play disguised as openness. Watch: Gemini 3.1 Ultra benchmarks vs GPT-5.4 Pro in production.

**Arm Made Its First Chip in 35 Years**
The 136-core, 3nm "AGI CPU" for AI inference launched with Meta as first customer, OpenAI, Cerebras, and Cloudflare signed up. Why it matters: Arm designing its own silicon (not just licensing) changes the chip competitive landscape. Direct competition with NVIDIA inference GPUs. Watch: benchmark results and how this affects NVIDIA's datacenter dominance narrative.

**Claude Gets Computer Use + ARC-AGI-3 Humiliates All Models**
Anthropic launched Computer Use in Cowork and Claude Code — Claude can now control your Mac (keyboard, mouse, apps) autonomously. Meanwhile, ARC-AGI-3 scored every frontier model under 1% on tasks humans solved easily. Gemini 3.1 Pro led at 0.37%. Grok 4.2: literally 0%. Why it matters: agentic capabilities are shipping to consumers while benchmarks reveal the gap between "impressive" and "general intelligence" remains vast.

**Sanders & AOC Propose Data Center Construction Ban**
Proposed banning all new data centers until Congress passes comprehensive AI regulation. Why it matters: most aggressive US AI policy position yet. Won't pass, but signals the political backlash is building. Watch: whether this influences zoning decisions at the state level.

## CRYPTO & DEFI

**BTC Slides to $66.5K — 5th Straight Losing Week for Equities**
Bitcoin dropped from ~$69.4K to $66.5K amid a macro-driven risk-off wave. The Dow entered correction territory (10% off peak). Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) paused its 13-week buying streak. Why it matters: BTC is trading as a risk asset again, tightly correlated with equities. The "digital gold" narrative is dormant. Watch: NFP data April 3 — a hot print could push BTC below $65K.

**Crypto Market Down ~45% From Oct 2025 Peak**
Senator Durbin cited a roughly 45% decline in overall crypto market value since October 2025 in arguing against crypto exposure for Social Security funds. ETH at ~$2,000, down from cycle highs. Why it matters: the bear market is officially here by traditional metrics. But institutional infrastructure (ETFs, Fannie Mae accepting crypto collateral) continues building underneath. Watch: whether institutional capital rotates from speculative to yield-generating DeFi.

**Hyperliquid (HYPE) Enters Top 10, Bucks the Trend**
HYPE hit ATH in active users and entered the top 10 by market cap at ~$40, up 500%+ from lows. Why it matters: in a bear market, the one perp DEX eating centralized exchange lunch is the signal. Hyperliquid is proving DeFi can compete on UX and speed, not just ideology. Watch: whether CEX competitors respond with fee cuts or token incentives.

**Prediction Markets Hit $24B Monthly Volume**
TRM Labs reports prediction market volume surged from $1.2B (early 2025) to $23.9B monthly. 840K unique wallets. Geopolitics now drives majority of activity over crypto price speculation. Kalshi valued at $11B. Why it matters: prediction markets are becoming the default interface for geopolitical risk pricing. This is crypto's killer app that isn't trying to be crypto. Watch: state-level regulatory pushback (Nevada already filed against Polymarket).

**Starknet Privacy Push — STRK20 + strkBTC**
Starknet introduced STRK20 (private token standard for anonymous ERC-20 transfers) and announced strkBTC for confidential Bitcoin DeFi. StarkWare co-founder publicly defended ZK tech against Canton/Solana rivalry. Why it matters: Starknet is betting privacy is the next L2 differentiator. If regulators don't kill it, this could attract serious institutional DeFi capital. Watch: 127M STRK unlock on April 15.

**Silo v3 Goes Live — Lending Reimagined**
Silo launched v3 with a new core assumption: collateral doesn't need to be sold to keep markets solvent. Lender protection first. Why it matters: if the mechanism works under stress, this is a genuine DeFi innovation, not just another fork. Watch: TVL growth and first liquidation events.

**Fannie Mae Accepting Crypto Collateral**
Institutional signal of the week: Fannie Mae now accepts crypto as collateral. Why it matters: this is the mortgage giant. When TradFi's most boring institutions start touching crypto, the infrastructure layer is complete regardless of what prices do.

## MACRO & MARKETS

**Iran Conflict Escalates — Oil Above $113/bbl**
Brent crude surged past $113 after Iran denied US talks were happening, killing deescalation hopes. Strait of Hormuz disruptions continue affecting 20% of global oil supply. Trump postponed strikes on Iranian power plants for 5 days, then the window closed. IDF says its Iran strike campaign is "nearly complete." Why it matters: this is the dominant macro force right now. Energy costs feed into everything — inflation, rate expectations, consumer spending. Watch: any ceasefire signals and April OPEC+ response.

**Dow Enters Correction — 5th Straight Losing Week**
S&P 500 down 5%+ in March. Dow shed 800 points Friday alone to enter correction (10% off peak). Amazon led decliners. Philadelphia Fed President warned inflation running above 2% makes her "more apprehensive about policy." Why it matters: the combination of war-driven oil spike + sticky inflation = no rate cuts anytime soon. The AI capex boom is running into a macro wall. Watch: whether tech earnings in April can break the losing streak.

**Bezos Raises $100B for "Project Prometheus" — AI Manufacturing**
Jeff Bezos is raising $100B to acquire manufacturing businesses and automate them with AI. JPMorgan, Goldman, Mizuho backing. Why it matters: this is the largest private investment vehicle ever assembled, and it's pointed at physical-world AI. If it works, it reshapes American manufacturing. If it doesn't, it's the most expensive AI experiment in history. Watch: first acquisition targets and union response.

**SoftBank Secures $40B Bridge Loan for OpenAI**
SoftBank took a $40B bridge loan (JPMorgan, Goldman, Mizuho, SMBC, MUFG) to fund further OpenAI investments. Runs to March 2027. Why it matters: SoftBank is now existentially coupled to OpenAI's success. This is Vision Fund 3 by another name. Watch: whether this signals an OpenAI IPO timeline acceleration.
