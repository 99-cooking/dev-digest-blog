## 🤖 AI & AGENTS

**Cerebras Files S-1, Targets May IPO**
The Nvidia challenger filed its public registration statement on April 17, revealing $510M in revenue and a $10B OpenAI inference deal. If it prices well, expect a flood of AI hardware companies to follow. **Watch:** SEC review timeline and whether the roadshow can sustain momentum with Middle East volatility.

**Stanford AI Index 2026: Capability Is Not Plateauing**
The annual report dropped this week with jaw-dropping numbers. SWE-bench coding scores went from 60% to near 100% in a single year. Organizational AI adoption hit 88%. Claude Opus 4.6 and Gemini 3.1 Pro now score above 50% on PhD-level science benchmarks. **Why it matters:** The "AI plateau" narrative is dead. Performance is accelerating on the hardest benchmarks. **Watch:** State-level AI regulation — US states passed a record 150 AI-related bills.

**NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super Goes Open Source**
NVIDIA released its 120B-parameter agentic model with full 51-page documentation — truly open weights, not the usual "open-ish" release. Built specifically for long-context agentic workloads. **Why it matters:** NVIDIA is playing both sides — selling chips AND giving away models that make those chips essential. Smart lock-in strategy. **Watch:** Adoption in enterprise agentic pipelines where efficiency matters more than raw capability.

**OpenAI Acquires Hiro Finance**
Announced April 14, the acquihire brings autonomous personal finance capabilities into ChatGPT. Direct competition with Anthropic's Claude financial connectors. Every AI lab now needs a fintech story.

**American Express Buys Hyper (Sam Altman-Backed)**
Amex acquired Hypercard, an agentic expense management startup backed by Altman, to power its commercial services automation. **Why it matters:** This is AI eating enterprise SaaS from the inside. Legacy finance giants buying agentic startups instead of building — expect this pattern to accelerate.

**Allbirds Pivots to... AI Compute**
The once-$4B shoe company announced it's rebranding as "NewBird AI" to become a GPU-as-a-Service company. Raising $50M. Stock popped $127M on the news. **Why it matters:** Peak AI bubble signal? When shoe companies pivot to GPU hosting, we're either in 1999 or building the future. Probably both. **Watch:** Stockholder vote on May 18.

**Anthropic Acquired Coefficient Bio ($400M)**
Anthropic's biggest acquisition to date — a life sciences startup — signals the company is betting heavily on AI for drug discovery and biotech. **Why it matters:** The lab wars are moving beyond chat. Whoever wins bio-AI wins a trillion-dollar market.

## 🪙 CRYPTO & DEFI

**Kelp DAO Exploited for $293M — Largest DeFi Hack of 2026**
An attacker drained 116,500 rsETH (~$293M) through Kelp DAO's LayerZero-powered cross-chain bridge on April 18. Took 46 minutes. DeFi TVL dropped from $26.4B to $20B overnight. AAVE fell 18%. **Why it matters:** Cross-chain bridges remain DeFi's Achilles heel. This exploit alone represents ~18% of rsETH's circulating supply. The contagion spread to protocols with zero direct exposure. **Watch:** Whether Kelp can recover funds or if this becomes another Ronin-style permanent loss.

**Morgan Stanley Launches First Major-Bank Bitcoin ETF**
MSBT began trading April 8 with a rock-bottom 0.14% expense ratio and pulled $100M in its first week. First spot BTC ETF issued by a major US bank. **Why it matters:** The institutional legitimation phase is complete. When Morgan Stanley — who once called Bitcoin "$0" — launches their own ETF, the narrative war is over. **Watch:** Goldman Sachs reportedly preparing their own product.

**Bitcoin Rallies to $75K on Iran Ceasefire Optimism**
BTC bounced from ~$60K lows to ~$75K as Middle East tensions eased. ETH followed, hitting $2,370 (+8% in a day). **Why it matters:** Crypto is now a macro asset — it trades on geopolitics, not just on-chain metrics. The correlation with risk-on equities is tighter than ever.

**StarkWare Restructures Amid 99% Revenue Collapse**
Monthly Starknet revenue dropped from $6M (late 2023) to $48K by April 2026. StarkWare is cutting staff and splitting into two business units. Bithumb paused STRK transactions for a mainnet upgrade. **Why it matters:** The L2 fee compression race has winners and losers. Starknet's tech is solid but usage hasn't followed. The restructuring is a survival move. **Watch:** Whether the mainnet upgrade and privacy features (Zcash-like "private Bitcoin") can reignite activity.

**CoW Swap Domain Hijacked for $1.2M**
Popular DEX aggregator lost $1.2M on April 14 via a domain hijacking attack — not a smart contract exploit but a web2 vulnerability. **Why it matters:** DeFi security isn't just about code audits. DNS, domains, and frontend attacks are the growing threat vector.

**DeFi Losses Hit $600M in 2026 So Far**
Between Kelp DAO, CoW Swap, Drift, and smaller exploits, total DeFi losses for 2026 are approaching $600M. **Why it matters:** At this pace, 2026 could rival 2022 for hack losses. Insurance protocols and formal verification are still afterthoughts.

## 🌍 MACRO & MARKETS

**Oil's Wild Week: $100 → $84 via Iran Drama**
Brent crude peaked at $118/bbl in March during the US-Iran conflict, fell to $95 on the April 7 ceasefire, spiked back above $100 on April 12 when talks collapsed and the US Navy blockaded the Strait of Hormuz, then crashed 12% to ~$84 after Iran declared Hormuz "completely open" during the Israel-Lebanon ceasefire. **Why it matters:** Energy price swings of this magnitude directly impact inflation expectations, central bank decisions, and tech company margins. **Watch:** Whether the Trump-brokered ceasefire holds. Negotiators are seeking a temporary memorandum, not a comprehensive deal.

**IMF Cuts Global Growth to 3.1%**
The April WEO report titled "Global Economy in the Shadow of War" cut 2026 growth by 0.2pp. Inflation revised up to 4.4%. Emerging markets face a 1.9pp hit in a severe conflict scenario — nearly double the advanced economy impact. **Why it matters:** The IMF is explicitly warning that markets may be ahead of fundamentals and a correction risk exists, especially in tech.

**Software Stocks Post Best Week Since 2001**
The IGV ETF climbed ~14% in five straight green sessions. Oracle +17%, Palantir, Microsoft, AMD, Salesforce all surging. Consumer Discretionary outperformed Defensive Staples by 700bp. **Why it matters:** Enterprise AI spending is the new consensus trade. BlackRock notes tech sector earnings growth projected at 43% for 2026 while forward valuations are at 2020 lows — a rare combination. **Watch:** Earnings season will test whether the AI revenue story is real.

**Gold Testing $4,900**
The ultimate hedge is still climbing despite the ceasefire. Investors aren't convinced the geopolitical calm is durable. **Why it matters:** When both gold and tech rally simultaneously, it signals liquidity is flowing everywhere — classic late-cycle behavior.
