## 🪙 CRYPTO NEWS

Starknet introduced strkBTC, a Bitcoin-based asset with Zcash-like privacy features. Shielded balances and confidential transfers using zero-knowledge proofs, while preserving full DeFi composability. Starknet Foundation says privacy is "a fundamental requirement for market participation" and aims to turn dormant BTC into an active financial asset.

[Crypto.news](https://crypto.news/vitalik-buterin-breaks-down-ethereum-strawmaps-plan-for-faster-slots-and-finality/)
